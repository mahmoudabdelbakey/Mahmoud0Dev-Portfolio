### Flagship Deep Dive: LogiCore Express

* **The Problem**: Morning dispatch reports took 14+ seconds, locking database tables and causing delivery route double-bookings.
* **Original Idea**: The client wanted a simple digital web form to replace paper trip slips.
* **How Mahmoud Improved It**: Recognized that paper wasn't the bottleneck — capacity calculation and reporting locks were. Proposed an automated dispatch engine with SQL stored procedures and spatial validation.
* **The Solution**: ASP.NET Core backend with snapshot isolation and pre-compiled SQL stored procedures.
* **Challenges Solved**: Eliminated order table lock contention during peak morning rushes.
* **The Result**:
  * **-85%** Query Latency (14.2s down to 210ms)
  * **Zero** Route booking conflicts
  * **40+ hours** Saved weekly by dispatchers
